MIIQAT-AI

Dataset Plan

No single public dataset contains Hajj crowds, shuttle telemetry, emergency routes, intervention outcomes and future-risk labels. Public data will train perception; controlled digital-twin data will evaluate prediction and interventions.

Recommended datasets and outputs

Module Recommended data Output
Hajj perception HAJJv2 Hajj scenes, abnormal movement and objects
Hajj counting HAJJv2-CrowdCount Dense-count accuracy and confidence
Traffic anomalies AI City Track 3 Traffic anomalies and explanations
Pedestrian safety AI City Track 2 Pedestrian–vehicle interactions
Vehicle flow CityFlow Multi-camera vehicle flow
Dense-crowd robustness UCF-QNRF Extreme-density robustness
Risk forecasting Custom digital-twin sequences Time-to-risk and propagation
Intervention testing Custom counterfactual scenarios Before/after total network risk

Custom scenario schema

timestamp, zone_id, capacity, crowd_count, inflow, outflow,
mean_speed, dominant_direction, bus_queue, emergency_route_open,
camera_confidence, gps_confidence, gate_confidence, heat_index,
intervention, downstream_risk, total_network_risk

Scenarios

Normal flow, rising inflow, opposing flow, shuttle delay, blocked emergency route, gate opening, pedestrian rerouting, shuttle throttling, camera failure and GPS failure.

Synthetic scenarios must be labelled simulated and never presented as recorded Hajj incidents.
